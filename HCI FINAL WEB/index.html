Things I Love Doing

Different hobbies, different vibes, but each one has a little piece of my personality.

Surfskating 🛹

Finding my flow, feeling the breeze, and enjoying the freedom of every ride. Surfskating is my kind of little escape—one push, one turn, and one new thing to learn.

It's about enjoying the ride, building confidence, and having fun while learning at my own pace. There's always another move to try!

Dancing 💃

Music on, worries off. Dancing lets me express myself without needing to explain anything. Sometimes, the best way to say something is simply to move to the beat.

Whether I'm learning a move or just enjoying a song, dancing gives me a chance to express my energy, creativity, and personality.

Gaming 🎮

More than just playing—it's strategy, teamwork, excitement, and a little friendly competition. Call of Duty: Mobile is also where my online name, Neira, found its beginning.

Gaming gives me a space to challenge myself, enjoy exciting matches, and connect with people who share the same interest. And of course, every gamer needs a name that feels like their own!
